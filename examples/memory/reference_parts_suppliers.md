Order of preference for parts:

1. **QBP** — account on file, next-day if ordered before 2pm, everything standard.
2. **The Hub on Sandy** — walk-in, ten minutes away, marked up but saves a day.
3. **Direct from manufacturer** — only for warranty replacements, two weeks.

Lead times that have actually held: QBP next-day is real. Manufacturer "5 to 7
business days" has never once been under two weeks.

**How to apply:** when a job is waiting on a part, the promise date uses the real
lead time, not the published one.
