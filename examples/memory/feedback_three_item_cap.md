Three items, maximum, in any plan or briefing. One that actually matters plus two
supporters.

**Why:** the first `/start` returned eleven open jobs, honestly and accurately. Dev
did none of them and did not open the assistant again for two days. Volume of
information is not the same as usefulness to a person who freezes on volume.

**How to apply:** rank, cut to three, and name the cut explicitly: "the other six
are parked, not lost." If something urgent is not in the three, it displaces one
rather than being added as a fourth.
