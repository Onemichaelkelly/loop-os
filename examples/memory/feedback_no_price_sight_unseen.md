Never produce a firm repair price for a bike nobody has looked at. Draft a range
with the inspection built in: "somewhere between X and Y, I will know once I see
the frame."

**Why:** in March, Dev quoted $180 over text for a tune-up and brake bleed. The
down tube had a crack. He either ate a $400 repair or looked like he was moving the
goalposts, and he ate it.

**How to apply:** any customer-facing draft containing a single dollar figure for
repair work gets stopped and rewritten as a range plus an inspection line. Parts
have fixed prices. Labor on an unseen bike does not.
