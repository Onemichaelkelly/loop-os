A wheel build is a three-day job in any customer-facing draft. Never same-day,
never next-day, no matter how the request is phrased.

**Why:** spokes need to settle and get re-tensioned. A wheel handed over the same
day comes back out of true in a month, and it comes back as a warranty job.

**How to apply:** applies to drafts, quotes, and calendar entries. If a customer
pushes, the honest line is "I can get it right in three days" rather than a
discount on the timeline.
